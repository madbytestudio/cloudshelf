Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate CloudShelf:

  • Account information: when you register, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password, handled by Supabase Auth. Your full name is stored if you provide it in the Profile page.
  • Registration verification: a one-time code is stored temporarily against your email address during sign-up. It is deleted once verified, and deleted with your account.
  • Device identifier: a one-way SHA-256 hash derived from a device identifier (Windows: machine UID and hostname; Android: ANDROID_ID), together with the device model name, platform, and installed app version. This record is stored on our servers in order to bind your account to one device and to prevent account sharing. The hash cannot be reversed back into the original identifier.
  • Subscription and payment records: when you subscribe to a book through CHIP (FPX), we store a sales record — the book, the amount paid, the date, the payment reference — plus the start and expiry dates of your access period. We never receive or store your online banking credentials.
  • Diagnostic data: if the App fails to open a book, it automatically sends a diagnostic report containing the app version, platform, device model, manufacturer, brand, product, hardware name, Android SDK level, the error message, and technical log text. This report contains no book content, no notes, and no personal text. It is used only to diagnose and fix technical faults.
  • Encrypted notes: your notes are encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device and stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Authenticate you and confirm your email address
  • Bind your account to a single device and prevent account sharing
  • Verify that you hold a valid access period for a book
  • Process subscriptions, renewals, and refund requests
  • Deliver app update notifications and service announcements by email
  • Diagnose and fix technical faults reported by the App
  • Derive the encryption keys that protect your downloaded books and notes on your device

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Encryption

All sensitive data uses encryption at rest:

  • Book files downloaded for offline use are encrypted with a device-bound key
  • User notes are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a separate key derivation salt
  • Encryption keys are derived via PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations and never leave your device

4. Third-Party Services

CloudShelf uses the following third-party services. Your use of those services is also subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage, and app update hosting. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • CHIP (FPX) — payment processing for book subscriptions. We never receive your online banking credentials or card details; all payment data is handled by CHIP. CHIP
  • Google (Gmail SMTP) — delivery of account verification codes, update notifications, and service emails. Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub — hosting and delivery of app update installers. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive — content delivery networks used to serve book files. Your IP address and request details are visible to the provider that serves your download, under that provider's privacy policy.
  • Tauri — desktop and mobile application framework. No data is sent to Tauri.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. CloudShelf provides a built-in account deletion feature accessible from the Profile page in the Reader app. When you delete your account, the following data is permanently wiped from our servers:

  • Your profile and login credentials
  • Your device binding records
  • Your book access permissions
  • Your diagnostic reports
  • Your pending registration verification codes and queued email records
  • Any books you have uploaded or created

Deleting your account also wipes the encrypted notes and the downloaded books stored on that device. In addition, downloaded copies of books whose access period has expired or been revoked are deleted automatically the next time the App connects to our servers.

To maintain financial and transaction records, sales data is retained even after account deletion. This includes the transaction reference, amount, date, and purchase history required for auditing and reconciliation. All other personal data is removed.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure authentication, one-device binding, and screen-capture protection for protected content. Note that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data. You can delete your account and all associated personal data at any time from the Profile page, or by contacting us at the email below.
